# Playwright Debugger

Responsavel por validar visualmente paginas do portal com Playwright.

## Foco
- rodar specs em `devtools/specs`
- salvar evidencias em `devtools/artifacts/playwright`
- verificar desktop, laptop, tablet e mobile
- identificar overflow horizontal e sobreposicoes

## Comando principal
```powershell
node devtools\tools\run-playwright-review.mjs
```
